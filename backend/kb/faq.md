# Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering & Payments

**How do I place an order?**
Add items to your cart, go to checkout, apply a coupon if you have one, and confirm. Payment is made from your in-app wallet balance, which you can top up from the Payments page. All prices are in PKR.

**What payment methods are accepted?**
Payments are made through your wallet balance. Top up your wallet from the Payments section, then pay for orders from that balance. We do not currently accept external cards or cash on delivery.

**Why did my payment fail?**
The most common reason is insufficient wallet balance. Top up your wallet to at least the order total and try again. If the item went out of stock while you were checking out, the payment will also fail — refresh your cart and retry.

**Can I use more than one coupon on an order?**
No. Only one coupon can be applied per order, and each coupon can be used once per customer. The discount is capped at the order total.

## Shipping & Delivery

**How long does delivery take?**
Standard delivery within major cities takes 2–4 business days. Remote areas may take 5–7 business days. You will see the current status of each order in your Order History.

**How much does shipping cost?**
Shipping charges depend on your city and the order weight. Standard shipping within major cities starts at PKR 200. Orders above PKR 5,000 qualify for free standard shipping.

**How do I track my order?**
Open Order History and select the order to see its current status: pending, paid, shipped, delivered, or cancelled. Tracking with live carrier updates is coming soon.

## Returns, Refunds & Warranty

**What is your return policy?**
You can request a return within 7 days of delivery for most items, provided they are unused and in original packaging. Some items (perishables, personal-care products, and clearance items) are non-returnable.

**How do refunds work?**
Once a return is approved, the refund is credited back to your wallet balance, usually within 3–5 business days. If you paid with a coupon, the coupon discount is not refunded in cash.

**How do I cancel an order?**
You can cancel an order yourself only before it has shipped (while it is pending or paid). Once shipped, contact support to request assistance. Cancelled orders are refunded to your wallet.

**Do products come with a warranty?**
Electronics carry a standard 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects unless otherwise stated on the product page. Keep your order ID as proof of purchase for any warranty claim.

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page, or contact support to have a reset link sent to your registered email.

**How do I update my profile or address?**
Go to your Account settings to update your profile details and delivery addresses.

**Do you have a loyalty program?**
Yes. You earn loyalty points on completed purchases, which can be redeemed on future orders. Check your points balance in your Account.
